## Detailed description

Screshot is a free screenshot tool for Chrome. Capture what you see or the whole page, mark it up, hide anything private, and paste it where you need it.

No account. No upload. Your screenshots stay on your computer.

CAPTURE
• Visible area: click the icon or press Ctrl+Shift+S
• Full page: right-click the icon → "Capture full page", or press Alt+Shift+S. Screshot scrolls and stitches the page for you, hides sticky headers so they don't repeat, and opens the result in its own tab so you can scroll through it, copy it or download it.
• Crop to any region, or double-click to keep the whole image

ANNOTATE
• Arrows, lines, rectangles, ellipses, pencil and highlighter
• Text with adjustable size
• Any color, unlimited undo/redo

BLUR
• Drag over emails, names, tokens or card numbers to hide them before you share
• Blurred areas can't be recovered from the exported image

SHARE
• Copy to clipboard and paste into Slack, Gmail, Jira, GitHub, Docs…
• Or download as PNG

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• activeTab: captures only the tab in front of you, only when you ask
• scripting: scrolls that tab for full-page capture, then restores it
• contextMenus: adds the right-click menu on the icon
No browsing history access, no background activity, no analytics in the editor.

Questions or ideas: https://www.screshot.com/contact
